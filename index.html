SHEET 01 / 07 — PROFILE

About

I'm interested in Intelligent Systems, Embodied AI, autonomous systems, and advanced perception — building adaptive, autonomous systems that can perceive, reason, learn, and interact effectively with dynamic environments.

I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science from NSUT Delhi and am currently pursuing an MSc in Robotics and Intelligent Systems at NTU Singapore, where I research uncertainty-aware 3D object tracking for robotic manipulation at the Robots Under Uncertainty Lab (RUNES), advised by Professor Lee JongSeok. My background spans 3D reconstruction (Gaussian Splatting, NeRF), object detection and tracking, and applied deep learning across academic and interdisciplinary research settings.
